WHO WE ARE

Your Partner in Construction & Engineering

COSEP Company Limited is a Kenyan-owned general building construction company established in 2015 and based in Nairobi, Kenya.

We provide integrated construction solutions, including professional building construction, design and build services, project management, and the supply of building materials.

Our work covers commercial, residential, industrial, civil construction, refurbishment and interior fit-out projects.
